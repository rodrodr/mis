# Design System: MIS · Municipal Ideological Score

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Boletim de Urna"**

Every figure on the site is printed as the receipt a Brazilian ballot box prints when the polls close, summed municipality by municipality. The ground is cool thermal paper laid on a slightly greyer counter. The ink is thermal black that fades to grey for anything secondary. Only two coloured inks exist: thermal red for the left and carbon-copy blue for the right. Numbers, labels, headers and controls are set in a monospace receipt face. Sentences use a hyperlegible companion face. The system rejects the dark dashboard of KPI tiles and the cream broadsheet.

Density is that of a printed slip: compact rows with tabular figures, hairline and dashed rules, and no ornament that a thermal printer could not make. State is printed as a mark, never as a colour. A provisional result gets a rubber stamp, imputed data gets a stamp, a municipality with too few votes is hatched, missing data is left blank, and a selection is bracketed, underlined with a wave or punched. Sections are separated by perforated tear lines. A section carries at most one monumental numeral and highlights the one line that matters.

The world has a dark theme ("carbon"), a more-contrast context and a reduced-motion context, all driven by the same tokens. The diverging map ramp is read from the tokens at runtime, so the map re-inks when the theme changes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Thermal paper on a counter: flat tonal layers, torn zigzag edges, dashed perforations.
- Hue is reserved for ideology. Red means left, blue means right, and the centre of the scale is paper grey.
- State is a mark: stamps, brackets, punched holes, hatching, blanks.
- Martian Mono prints every number and label. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next carries every sentence.
- At most one monumental numeral per section, in light-weight mono at up to 6rem.

## Colors

The palette is ink on thermal paper, plus two ideological inks on a diverging scale. Every other role is a grey.

### Primary
- **Thermal Ink** (`text-primary`, `fig-ink`, `accent-solid`, `focus-ring`; oklch(0.225 0.012 258)): body text, chart marks and outlines, the receipt's typical-municipality dot, active tab underline, links, focus rings and pressed buttons. The system's accent is ink itself, because hue is spoken for.

### Secondary
- **Thermal Red** (`ideo-left`, oklch(0.54 0.18 27); `ideo-left-strong`, oklch(0.46 0.165 27); `ideo-left-soft`, oklch(0.81 0.09 25)): the left half of the diverging scale only. Strong marks the far end and is used for outlines and map extremes. Soft is the near-centre fill.

### Tertiary
- **Carbon-Copy Blue** (`ideo-right`, oklch(0.52 0.15 264); `ideo-right-strong`, oklch(0.44 0.14 265); `ideo-right-soft`, oklch(0.81 0.075 262); `ideo-right-faint`, oklch(0.95 0.018 262)): the right half of the scale, mirroring the red steps. The faint step is the first band right of centre in the ideology-band chart.

### Neutral
- **Thermal Paper** (`bg-raised`, oklch(0.972 0.003 230)): the printed slip. Used for the receipt, header, figures, map canvas, side panel, footer, inputs, buttons, and the hollow dots of the electorate average.
- **Counter Grey** (`bg-base`, oklch(0.948 0.004 230)): the page ground the slips sit on. Also the centre of the ideology scale (`ideo-center`), so a neutral municipality reads as unprinted paper.
- **Paper Shadow** (`bg-surface`, oklch(0.905 0.005 230)): the "not significant" class in LISA maps and data cells.
- **Faded Ink** (`text-secondary`, `fig-axis`; oklch(0.47 0.009 245)): subtitles, legends, sources, axes and the zero line.
- **Tear Grey** (`paper-tear`, oklch(0.7 0.007 235)): perforations, dashed borders and dotted table rules.
- **Paper Edge** (`paper-edge`, oklch(0.84 0.006 230)): the 1px edge under a torn receipt. It shares its value with **Blank** (`fig-missing`), the fill for missing data.
- **Hatch Grey** (`fig-hatch`, oklch(0.58 0.008 240)): the 45° hatching for municipalities with fewer than 300 female votes, and the stamp-free "few votes" mark.
- **Control Grey** (`border-control`, oklch(0.58 0.008 240)): input, button and dropdown borders at ≥3:1 against the page ground.
- **Gridline** (`fig-grid`, `border-subtle`; oklch(0.905 0.005 230)): chart gridlines, receipt strip baselines and tick marks.

In the dark theme every role resolves to its `-dark` sibling in the frontmatter. The paper becomes carbon (`bg-raised-dark` on `bg-base-dark`). The inks lighten, and the strong and soft steps swap so that the extremes of the scale stay the highest-contrast marks against the dark ground. In the more-contrast context, secondary text, axes and hatching all collapse to full ink.

### Named Rules
**The Two Inks Rule.** Hue means ideology and nothing else. Thermal red is left and carbon-copy blue is right, on a diverging scale that passes through paper grey. Gender, office, arena, selection and status never get a hue.

**The State Is a Mark Rule.** A data state is printed, never coloured. Provisional and imputed data get a stamp. Few votes gets hatching. Missing data is blank paper grey. A selection is shown with a bracket, a wavy underline, a 2px ink border or a punched hole.

**The Ink Is the Accent Rule.** Links, focus rings, active tabs and pressed buttons use thermal ink. The kit's status reds, greens and ambers are not part of this world.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Martian Mono Variable (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace)
**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Variable (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono Variable, the same face as the display role. The machine prints the numbers and labels; a person writes the sentences.

**Character:** A wide, squared receipt monospace with slashed zeros, paired with a humanist grotesque built for legibility. The slashed zeros are deliberate. Both faces ship them as legibility glyphs, and they keep 0 apart from O in IBGE codes. Both faces are self-hosted variable fonts and preloaded.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Martian Mono 300, `display`, lh 1, −0.04em, tabular figures): the monumental numeral, such as the 2026 typical-municipality score, the female share or the peak Dalton index. At most one per section. A small uppercase mono label above it names what it measures, and a stamp beside it marks provisional data. Secondary readouts in the map panel and the methodology formula reuse the numeral style at step 4 (2.07–2.75rem) through inline overrides, not a role of their own.
- **Headline** (Martian Mono 700, `headline`, lh 1.1, −0.01em, uppercase): page titles (MAP, POLARIZATION, GENDER…). The municipality profile appends its state code in mono at step 2.
- **Title** (Atkinson 700, `title`, lh 1.1): narrative step headings and section headings inside prose. Figure titles use the same face and weight one step down (`figure-title`), and they state the answer rather than the topic.
- **Body** (Atkinson 400, `body`, lh 1.55): all running prose. The prose measure is about 57ch (`measure-reading` 46 × 1.25ch). The lede is one step up and capped at 60ch. Long prose hyphenates only in Spanish, and never splits acronyms, DOIs or URLs.
- **Label** (Martian Mono 400, `label`, 0.04em, uppercase): navigation, control labels, search label, button text and the numeral's label. `label-small` (0.05em) sets receipt section headers, control legends, legends and the scale ends.
- **Receipt** (Martian Mono 400, `mono`, tabular figures): receipt rows, the map panel, data tables, chart text and the BibTeX block.
- **Stamp** (Martian Mono 700, `stamp`, 0.12em, uppercase): rubber stamps only.

### Named Rules
**The Receipt Type Rule.** If the machine would print it (a number, a code, a label, a header, a control), it is set in Martian Mono. If a person would say it as a sentence, it is set in Atkinson. Uppercase is allowed only in mono labels, page titles and stamps (a registered exception in PRODUCT.md), never in running prose.

**The One Numeral Rule.** Each section carries at most one monumental numeral. It is the reading that matters. Every other number stays at receipt size.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every number in a row, table, list, ranking or readout uses tabular lining figures, so the columns line up like a printed tally.

## Layout

The page shell is a single column capped at 90rem (`size-page`). It has a fluid side gutter (`spacing.s`), `spacing.m` above the content and `spacing.2xl` below it. Spacing is a fluid clamped scale from `3xs` to `2xl` that grows about 12% from mobile to desktop. Vertical rhythm comes from stacks, with `spacing.s` as the default gap. Sections are separated by a perforated tear line with `spacing.xl` above and below.

- **Header:** sticky thermal-paper strip with a 1px dashed tear line underneath. Brand (MIS + uppercase name), the mono nav, then language and theme controls on one row. At ≤60rem the nav drops to its own full-width row that scrolls horizontally, and the brand name is hidden.
- **Front page (portada):** at ≥60rem, two columns. The receipt sits on the left (20–27rem); the numeral, search and the 2026 map share the right. Below 60rem the numeral, search and map come first and the receipt follows.
- **Narrative (relato):** at ≥60rem, prose steps (1.4fr) beside a sticky figure (1fr) that swaps state per step. Each step is at least 60svh tall. Without JS every state is shown stacked.
- **Map explorer:** at ≥64rem, the map with a 20rem side panel. Controls wrap above the map.
- **Footer:** authors and citation, 1fr/2fr at ≥50rem.
- Every grid child sets `min-inline-size: 0`, so nothing overflows at 320px.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth is tonal: thermal-paper slips (`bg-raised`) lie on the counter-grey page (`bg-base`). There are no box-shadows. The kit's `shadow-raised` token is not used. The only cast edge is the torn receipt: a 1px paper-edge drop-shadow under its zigzag mask, which makes the slip read as paper with thickness. Overlays (the search listbox, the figure note) separate by border or inverse colour, not by lift.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Paper edge** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 1px 0 var(--color-paper-edge))`): only under a torn receipt.

### Named Rules
**The Paper-on-Counter Rule.** Something sits above the page only because it is a separate slip of paper, never because it floats. Lift is shown with tone and torn edges, never with blur.

## Shapes

Paper is square. The only corner radius is 4px (`rounded.sm`), on inputs, buttons, stamps and the figure note. Receipts have torn top and bottom edges: a repeating zigzag mask of 12 × 6px teeth. Perforations are dashed: 2px dashed tear grey between sections and above figures and the footer, and 1px dashed under the header and around year-rail buttons and the search input. Table rows are separated by dotted rules. Data dots are circles: a filled ink dot for the typical municipality and a hollow ring for the electorate. Stamps are rotated −4° and have a double rule (a 2px border plus a 1px outline offset by 2px). The punched hole is a small circle in the page colour, outlined in ink, that sits on the selected year.

## Components

### Buttons
Squared paper tickets. They read as controls on a printed slip, not as pills.
- **Shape:** gently squared (4px) with a hairline control-grey border.
- **Default:** thermal paper with ink text in uppercase mono at label size. Height 2.75rem, padding `spacing.s` left and right.
- **Pressed / toggled** (`aria-pressed="true"`, e.g. Play): inverts to ink with paper text.
- **Focus:** a 2px ink focus ring, offset 2px. Links styled as buttons share the same treatment.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Search (the receipt's input line):** thermal paper with a 1px *dashed* control-grey border, 4px corners, mono text, 2.75rem tall, under an uppercase mono label. The results listbox is a paper sheet with a hairline control border. The active option is outlined 2px in ink (inset), not tinted.
- **Selects:** thermal paper, hairline control border, 4px, mono, under `label-small` legends.

### Navigation
Uppercase mono links in ink, no underline, each at least 2.75rem tall. The current page gets a hairline *wavy* underline offset 0.35em. The current language is bold and bracketed: `[EN] PT ES`. The theme control is an unstyled mono text button (THEME: SYSTEM / LIGHT / DARK).

### Receipt (signature)
The *boletim*. It is a thermal-paper slip with torn edges and mono text at receipt size. It has a centred uppercase header, then one block per office (FEDERAL DEPUTY, then CITY COUNCIL), each opened by a 2px dashed tear line and an uppercase `label-small` heading. Each election is one row: year, a printed −0.5…+0.5 strip, value. The strip has a hairline baseline with quarter ticks and a dotted zero line. A filled ink dot marks the typical municipality and a hollow ring marks the electorate. A provisional row ends with an asterisk. A solid ink rule closes the slip above the left/right scale legend. The arenas are stacked rather than set side by side, as a real boletim prints one block per office. The rows are compacted so all 17 fit the desktop first viewport. Older rows are never faded, because that would misrepresent data quality. The same slip frames the Methodology contents and the Data file list. Motion: v1 ships no animation. The brief's receipt-printing feed (rows printing out line by line in ≤400ms) is not built. The map's Play button steps one election every 1.4s. The kit's duration, easing and reduced-motion tokens exist, but no surface uses them yet.

### Stamps
Rubber stamps for data state: PROVISIONAL, IMPUTED, EXPERIMENTAL, plus source stamps such as TSE and v2.0. They use `stamp` type in ink, a 2px border plus a 1px outline offset 2px, 4px corners, and a −4° rotation. A stamp always sits next to a short plain-language note.

### Year Rail
The election picker on the map. There are two rows, GEN and MUN. Each year is a small paper ticket with a 1px dashed tear border, at least 24px tall. The selected year gets a 2px solid ink border, bold text and a punched hole at its top edge.

### Figures
A figure is a thermal-paper slip with a 2px dashed perforation along its top. The title is in Atkinson bold and states the answer. The subtitle gives the question and the unit, in faded ink. Chart, Table and Data views are uppercase mono tabs, and the selected tab has a 2px ink underline. Chart text is mono. Legends use mono at `label-small` with small swatches. The diverging ramp bar has a hairline axis border, with mono end labels (−0.5 Left · 0 · Right +0.5). Arena is encoded by stroke, never by hue: general elections use a solid line with filled dots, municipal elections a dashed line (5 4) with hollow dots. CSV and SVG downloads are buttons.

### Figure Note (tooltip)
An inverse slip: ink background with paper text, 4px corners, label size, tabular figures, at most 16rem wide. It opens on hover and on focus, can be traversed by keyboard, and never times out.

### Map Panel & Tables
The side panel is a thermal-paper sheet in mono. It holds a definition list with uppercase `label-small` terms and tabular values, a histogram in ink, and the legend. Long tables are mono at `label-small` size with dotted tear-grey row rules, a sticky paper header, and right-aligned tabular numbers.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** design every map figure-first: the map fits the viewport, and its context (measure, year, arena, provisional stamp) and its key are printed inside the map frame, in a column (desktop) or strips (mobile) that the projection keeps free. A reader or a screenshot never sees the map without knowing what and when it shows, and no label or key covers data. Animation (Play) runs within one arena and never moves the geometry, only the colour.
- **Do** make every chart fit the viewport with its title, key and controls: chart height is capped at the space left under the header (`.figura … svg` max-block-size), and faceted charts show each arena in its own panel with only its own elections (no empty rows).
- **Do** colour parties by ideological group, never by party: seven equal bands of the −1…+1 scale (FL/EE/EI … FR/ED/ED) on a red → green → blue ramp (`--color-ideo-grupo-*`, user-requested; green marks the centre only in this party palette), every bar outlined in ink.
- **Do** let readers isolate one series in many-line charts: hover, keyboard focus or a tap highlights it (thicker ink, bold label) and turns every other line, point and label `--color-fig-muted`; a tap pins it, Esc or a second tap releases it.
- **Do** use filters instead of overlaying series (one arena at a time, one election at a time) and label lines directly at their ends.
- **Do** place a map's colour key above or beside it, never below the fold; every mark used (hatching, no data) is explained at every width.

### Do:
- **Do** print every number, code and label in Martian Mono, with tabular lining figures wherever numbers sit in rows.
- **Do** mark data state with a printed mark: a −4° double-ruled stamp for provisional, imputed or experimental data, 45° hatching (1px lines at 4px pitch) for fewer than 300 female votes, and blank paper grey for missing data.
- **Do** separate sections with a perforated tear line (2px dashed `paper-tear`, `spacing.xl` above and below).
- **Do** give a section at most one monumental numeral (Martian Mono 300, up to 6rem, −0.04em) with an uppercase mono label above it.
- **Do** draw ideology on the diverging scale from `ideo-left-strong` through `ideo-center` to `ideo-right-strong`, reading the colours from the tokens at runtime so the dark theme re-inks the map.
- **Do** mark the current or selected item with a bracket, a wavy underline, a 2px ink border or a punched hole.
- **Do** encode arena and average by stroke and fill: solid versus dashed lines, filled dot for the typical municipality, hollow ring for the electorate.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use red or blue for anything other than left and right. That rules out women versus men, mayor versus governor, selection markers and status messages.
- **Don't** add box-shadows, floating cards, or corners rounder than 4px on surfaces and controls. Paper is square and lies flat.
- **Don't** set running prose in uppercase, and don't set long-form prose (prose blocks, ledes, narrative steps) in mono. Uppercase is limited to mono labels, page titles and stamps.
- **Don't** fade older receipt rows or tint state. A faded row reads as worse data.
- **Don't** put the general and municipal arenas on one continuous line or in one series.
- **Don't** use glyph icons as control labels. Name the control in words.
- **Don't** build the dark dashboard of KPI tiles or the cream broadsheet. Both are the thesis's named anti-references.
