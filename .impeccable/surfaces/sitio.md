Scope: whole site `sitio/` — Evolution (home, Read; signature moment Experience), Map / Profile / Comparator (Operate), Polarization, Gender, Methodology, Data (Read). Three languages, EN source and root.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every figure is printed as the receipt the ballot box prints at close — the *boletim de urna* — summed municipality by municipality. Refuses the dark dashboard of KPI tiles and the cream broadsheet.

OWN-WORLD: Cool thermal-paper white ground, thermal-black ink fading to grey, two inks only for ideology: thermal red (left) and carbon-copy blue (right) on a diverging scale; perforated tear lines between sections; monospace receipt type for numbers, labels and headers plus a readable companion face for prose; rubber stamps (PROVISÓRIO, IMPUTADO); QR-like data blocks. State is a mark, not a hue (raise from cutting bench): selection = punched hole/bracket, provisional = stamp, imputed = dotted print, few votes = hatching, missing = torn/blank.

STORY: The reader sees Brazil's receipt — 17 elections printed as successive rows (raise from sampler: whole chronological rows, two columns general/municipal) — understands the two arenas and the left–right movement, then tears off to the map, a municipality's own receipt, polarization and gender.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A tall receipt fills the left half: header "MIS · 1994–2026 · 5.571 MUNICÍPIOS", then 17 printed rows, each with the national mean as a dot on a printed −1…+1 scale, general and municipal columns; one monumental numeral (raise from storm) states the 2026 reading with a PROVISÓRIO stamp. Right half: the 2026 map printed in two inks. Primary action: "Find your municipality" search printed as the receipt's input line.

FORM: Boletim de Urna, position 3 of my ordered list (1 IBGE atlas plates, 2 Concretismo gradation, 3 boletim de urna, 4 Aloísio Magalhães identity programs, 5 BLS questionnaire scale, 6 Câmara hemiciclo, 7 apuração broadcast). ## Recorded deviations (finish review, round 1)
- Receipt arenas are stacked, not side by side: a real boletim de urna prints one block per office (DEPUTADO FEDERAL, then VEREADOR), and at 27rem two columns would shrink the scale strips below legibility. Rows were compacted so all 17 fit the desktop first viewport.
- Slashed zeros are the built-in legibility glyphs of both faces (Martian Mono, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next); kept deliberately.
- Thermal ink fade, QR blocks: not adopted in v1 (fading older rows would misrepresent data quality; QR adds no function on screen).
